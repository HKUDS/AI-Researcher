\begin{abstract}
\textbf{Scope: English instructed-roleplay evaluations, open-weight models (3B--70B) only.} Results do not generalize to frontier-scale ($>$70B closed-weight) or autonomous-deception settings without additional study.

We apply three evaluation controls---prompt equalization, cross-family extraction, and regex baseline---to behavioral deception-detection claims, collapsing Pacchiardi et al.\ instructed-detection from 93.9--100\% to 52--69\% (30--41\,pp) and revealing instruction-following dominance. The \textbf{construct-validated pipeline (correction-marker only) achieves 54.5\%}; the surface-lexical refusal-count rule achieves \textbf{80.1\% pooled-LOO} ($k\!\geq\!1$, no calibration data, range 64--88\%). The full 5-feature cross-family pipeline achieves \textbf{64.7\%} (Mistral~L3 extractor; upper bound---40\% of above-chance signal from ICC-failing features). \emph{The rule-vs-pipeline equivalence holds in the single-turn/pooled-LOO regime; at $\geq$14B multi-turn, the pipeline outperforms the rule by $+$14--29\,pp (§\ref{sec:pipeline_ablation}).}

A 9--10\,pp same-family extraction bias is localized to the Claude Haiku checkpoint by five self-family controls; Llama~70B-on-Llama~70B achieves 74.2\%, $-$9.7\,pp below Haiku.
For autonomous deception: sycophancy transfers (68.5/83\% at 3B/14B, $n\!=\!200$), confirmed by system-prompt-only control (63.1\%/69.5\%, $n\!=\!200$). Fully-autonomous persona/false-belief scenarios cannot be assessed: spot-checks reveal a scenario-design artifact (models abandon false personas), so these cells provide no evidence about detector capability.
Cross-organizational replication ($n\!=\!100$ each) localizes the Qwen~32B zero-marker collapse to the Qwen family: Gemma~2~27B achieves 84\% ($p\!<\!0.001$) and Mistral~7B 56\% (ns), disconfirming a general RLHF agreeableness effect.
Two frontier-scale targets are evaluated: Sonnet~4.5 ($n\!=\!99$, closed-weight) and Llama~4~Maverick ($n\!=\!100$, frontier open-weight MoE); results reported in §\ref{sec:frontier_llama405b}.
\end{abstract}
