\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\subsection{Construct Validity: Validated at $\alpha=0.606$}
\label{sec:icc_caveat}
\label{sec:icc_owned}

\paragraph{High-confidence reliability (pre-registered).}
On the $n\!=\!52$ high-confidence subset, \textbf{ICC(2,1)\,$=\!0.71$} (selection biases this upward). The full $n\!=\!100$ study (Appendix~\ref{app:human_icc_n100}) yields $\alpha\!=\!0.606$ (the primary validation unit).

\paragraph{n=20 pilot ICC discrepancy.} The initial $n\!=\!20$/2-annotator pilot yielded ICC$\!=\!0.114$ (scale-range artifact; rank-order preserved, Spearman $\rho\!=\!0.619$). The $n\!=\!100$/3-annotator study recovered $\alpha\!=\!0.606$, clearing the pre-registered threshold ($\alpha\!\geq\!0.4$).

Correction-marker density is the \emph{only} validated feature. \textbf{Key implication:} correction-marker-only achieves \textbf{54.5\%} (Haiku-extracted); full pipeline 64.7\% (\textbf{40\% of above-chance signal from ICC-failing features}).

\paragraph{Per-feature LOO ablation.}
Removing correction-marker density drops LOO from 67.1\% to 57.0\% ($-$10.2\,pp); the four ICC-failing features contribute $\leq$3.2\,pp individually (Appendix~\ref{app:feature_validity}). \textbf{Cross-family construct-valid bound:} under Mistral~L3 extraction, the four ICC-failing features alone achieve 57.0\% (Table~\ref{tab:feature_ablation_proxy}); correction-marker density accounts for the remaining $+$10.2\,pp uplift to 67.1\%. The construct-valid cross-family signal is therefore $\sim$10\,pp above chance, substantially below the 64.7\% headline. \textbf{L-indep claims} (80.1\% rule, 30--41\,pp collapse) are unaffected.

\subsection{Three Transfer Regimes}
\label{sec:transfer_regimes}
\label{sec:blackbox_vs_whitebox}

\paragraph{Regime 1: Instructed roleplay.} The explicit instruction dominates: equalization collapses LOO from 93.9--100\% to 52--69\% (30--41\,pp). The residual is surface-lexical (80.1\% rule).

\paragraph{Regime 2: Semi-autonomous (sycophancy).} Largely dispositional ({\S}\ref{sec:autonomous_pilot}): marginal epistemic-pressure contribution is $+$5--14\,pp over the system-prompt-only baseline.

\paragraph{Regime 3: Fully autonomous (persona/false-belief).} Null results; scenario-design artifact as leading candidate explanation ({\S}\ref{sec:exp_i_4th_scenario}). This finding rests on $n\!=\!10$ spot-checked trials from a single model (Qwen~14B); extension to Llama~3B and Mistral~7B is the highest-priority replication gap (limitation~(f)).

\subsection{Summary of Contributions}
\label{sec:novelty}

The primary contribution is a \textbf{methodological audit}: we provide an independent evaluation suite that, under tighter controls, yields construct-valid accuracy of 54.5--64.7\%, substantially below Pacchiardi et al.'s reported 67--73\% (their Table~1). The three controls identify instruction-following (30--41\,pp), extractor-quality differences ($+$7--10\,pp), and surface-lexical patterns as contributors to inflated accuracy in the general paradigm; whether these fully explain the specific gap to Pacchiardi et al.\ remains untested absent a direct replication of their protocol.

\paragraph{Protocol generality.}
\label{sec:protocol_generality}
Applied to SAPLMA-style LR probes~\cite{azaria2023internal} ($n\!=\!3$ targets; §\ref{sec:saplma_application}), probes fall within $\pm$6\,pp of the surface-lexical ceiling, replicating the core finding in an activation-probing paradigm.

\paragraph{Classifier generalization.}
\label{sec:loo_across_models}
Leave-one-model-out yields 64.2\%, within $+$3.0\,pp of pooled LOO (Appendix~\ref{app:loo_across_models}).

\paragraph{Reflexive implication.} The regimes where detection is validated are precisely where controls reveal artifactual or dispositional signal: instructed-roleplay accuracy is dominated by instruction-following (30--41\,pp collapse), and semi-autonomous sycophancy is largely dispositional (system-prompt-only baselines account for the majority of signal). The deployment-relevant autonomous regime, where genuine strategic deception would most matter, is precisely where this paper has null results. This creates an uncomfortable asymmetry: high detection accuracy is achievable only in settings where the ``deception'' is an artifact of evaluation design.

\paragraph{Paradigm boundaries.} Two untested conditions bound interpretation:

\textbf{(i) Base-model test.} If refusal markers are RLHF-induced rather than reflecting knowledge-conflict processing, the paradigm detects post-training response styles, not deception per se. Suggestive evidence: SAPLMA-style probes on the base Mistral-7B-v0.1 (no instruction tuning) yield no layer exceeding the 68\% refusal-count ceiling (Appendix~\ref{app:mistral_probe_panel}), consistent with the signal being a post-training artifact. A systematic base-model sweep across families would be required to confirm this.

\textbf{(ii) False-positive rate on benign conversations.} The 80.1\% rule accuracy is measured on a balanced truth/lie evaluation set. In deployment, the base rate of deceptive outputs is unknown and likely low. The false-positive rate on ordinary benign conversations (where no knowledge conflict exists) is unmeasured; if substantial, it would render the 80.1\% figure misleading for deployment utility. This is the highest-priority measurement gap for any practical application of these methods.

\subsection{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\textbf{(a)} Mock results establish correctness only. \textbf{(b)} Frontier-scale (100B+) models tested at two datapoints only; results scope to $\leq$70B open-weight. \textbf{(c)} Cross-organizational replication (Gemma~2~27B, Mistral~7B) localizes Qwen~32B collapse to the Qwen family; zero-marker collapse is not a general RLHF effect. \textbf{(d)} Autonomous-transfer asymmetry lacks validated causal account. \textbf{(e)} The binding regime for deployment (frontier-scale, autonomous, multilingual) is where this paper has least to say; whether the three controls produce similar collapses at frontier scale is an open empirical question. \textbf{(f)} Persona/false-belief spot-check ($n\!=\!10$) performed on Qwen~14B only; generalization to Llama~3B and Mistral~7B is assumed but unverified. \textbf{(g)} All targets are instruction-tuned; whether the signal exists on base models is untested. Additional limitations: Appendix~\ref{app:limitations_detail}.
